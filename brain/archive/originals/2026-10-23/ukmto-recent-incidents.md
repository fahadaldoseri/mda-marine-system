### Attack UKMTO #179 — 21 October 2026
UKMTO WARNING 179-26 - ATTACK · Incident Date: 21 Oct 2026 · Incident Time: 0145UTC · Local Authorities
UKMTO has received a report of an incident 11NM west of Bukha, Oman. Local Authorities have reported that a Tanker has been struck by an unknown projectile which started a fire which has been extinguished. The Tanker is now at anchor. All crew are reported as safe. There is no reported environmental impact at this time.

### Attack UKMTO #180 — 20 October 2026
UKMTO WARNING 180-26 - ATTACK · Incident Date: 20 Oct 2026 · Incident Time: 1830UTC · Source: Master
UKMTO has received a time late report of an incident 30NM southeast of Al Faw, Iraq. The Master of a tanker reports being struck by an unknown projectile, the crew are reported as safe and there is no reported damage or environmental impact.

### Advisory UKMTO #178 — 20 October 2026
UKMTO has received a report of an incident involving a tanker and military forces approximately 121NM east of Duqm, Oman. Reports indicate that the tanker was subject to interaction as part of ongoing military activity in the region. Authorities are aware and relevant investigations remain ongoing.
