## UKMTO #175 — 16 October 2026 (Attack)
UKMTO has received a report of an incident 78NM northeast of Al Jubayl, Saudi Arabia. The Master of a tanker has reported being attacked by an RPG from a small boat, resulting in damage to the aft port quarter. Crew are accounted for and reported safe. A complete damage assessment and environmental impact are unknown at time of report. Vessels are advised to transit with caution and report any suspicious activity to UKMTO, whilst authorities investigate.

## UKMTO #174 — 16 October 2026 (Attack)
UKMTO has received a time-late report of an incident 68NM northeast of Al Jubayl, Saudi Arabia of a tanker stuck by an unknown projectile. All crew are reported safe and have been evacuated. There is no reported environmental impact at time of report. Vessels are advised to transit with caution and report any suspicious activity to UKMTO while authorities are investigating.
