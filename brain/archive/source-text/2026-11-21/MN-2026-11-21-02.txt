UKMTO WARNING 213-26 - SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY Incident Date: 20 Nov 2026 Incident Time: 1102UTC UKMTO has received a report of an incident 95NM southwest of Al Mukalla, Yemen. A Bulk Carrier travelling eastbound in the IRTC was approached on the port side by a dark blue skiff with 6 unidentified, armed persons on board. Four shots were fired in quick succession from the skiff and warning shots were returned by the armed security team onboard the Bulk Carrier.
