UKMTO has received a time-late report of an incident 6NM north of Khasab, Oman. A tanker was struck by an unknown projectile in the engine room while transiting outbound through the Strait of Hormuz. The crew are safe and accounted for. Damage assessment, and environmental impact are unknown at the time of this report. Vessels are advised to transit with caution and report any suspicious activity
