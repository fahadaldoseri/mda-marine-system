## Operational Guidance
**Required actions:**
- Raising readiness when transiting the Strait of Hormuz, especially in the stretch between 6 and 13 nautical miles north of Khasab where strikes have recurred.
- Reviewing the latest UKMTO reports before every transit, without relying on media headlines alone.
- Preparing firefighting equipment on tankers before entering the strait.
- Watching for approaching small boats in the recommended transit corridor in the Gulf of Aden and the Arabian Sea, and reporting any suspicious approach to UKMTO immediately.
- Following any official warning on the Saudi ports on the Red Sea before heading to them.

## Event Log
- [Fact | MN-2026-11-21-01] **Tanker struck north of Khasab, warning 214-26 — 20/11/2026:** UKMTO announced that a tanker outbound from the Strait of Hormuz was struck on its port bow by a projectile from an unknown party, 8 nautical miles north of Khasab in the Sultanate of Oman, at 1500UTC. The company security officer reported a fire on board the tanker, and no casualties or environmental impact were recorded. Source: [UKMTO](https://www.ukmto.org/recent-incidents#a9279ed0-9fba-45aa-8139-29871a936e20).
- [Fact | MN-2026-11-21-02] **Suspicious activity southwest of Al Mukalla, warning 213-26 — 20/11/2026:** UKMTO announced that a dark blue small boat carrying 6 armed persons approached the port side of an eastbound bulk carrier in the recommended transit corridor. This took place 95 nautical miles southwest of Al Mukalla in Yemen, at 1102UTC. 4 shots in quick succession were fired from the boat, the armed security team on the vessel responded with warning shots, and the boat then moved away. The master reported that the vessel was not damaged and no crew member was injured, and it is continuing its voyage. Source: [UKMTO](https://www.ukmto.org/recent-incidents#a4bc99f3-ccc2-469c-ab05-53d2009c8974).
- [Unconfirmed report | MN-2026-11-21-03] **Houthi warning to shipping companies at Saudi ports — 20/11/2026:** The gCaptain site relayed from Reuters that the Houthi military spokesperson Yahya Saree demanded that workers near the Saudi ports on the Red Sea evacuate them, and urged shipping companies to move their vessels out of them immediately. The warning came after the Saudi-led coalition announced that it had struck the port of Hodeidah. The text of the warning does not appear in any official source checked, and no report of an attack on a vessel in the Red Sea was issued as of 0340Z. Source: [gCaptain](https://gcaptain.com/houthis-order-ships-to-leave-saudi-red-sea-ports-after-hodeidah-airstrikes/).
