# Daily Maritime Security Report
> Maritime Domain Awareness Centre — Kingdom of Bahrain · 28/11/2026 · UTC

## Executive Summary
- [Fact] On 27/11 the US Central Command announced that on 26/11 it disabled a Palau-flagged dry bulk carrier, the "Pacific Archer", in the Gulf of Oman. It stated that this followed the vessel's departure from Bandar Abbas and that its civilian crew was unharmed. It added that US forces had redirected 158 vessels and disabled 6 that refused to comply.
- [Fact] On 26/11 UKMTO issued advisory 224-26 on an incident involving a cargo vessel and military forces in the Gulf of Oman, without naming the vessel. No new attack reports appeared on the UKMTO page after the two reports of 25/11.
- [Evidence pending] On 27/11 and 28/11 Iranian agencies, namely Mehr, Press TV and IRNA, reported that the Revolutionary Guard announced the explosion of two tankers near the Strait of Hormuz after they struck sea mines. Neither UKMTO nor the US Central Command issued anything confirming this.
