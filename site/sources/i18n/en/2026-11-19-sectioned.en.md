## Operational Guidance
**Do:**
- Raise readiness when transiting the Strait of Hormuz, especially in the stretch between 6 and 13 nautical miles north of Khasab.
- Review the latest UKMTO reports before every transit, and do not rely on media headlines alone.
- Report any strike, floating object or suspicious activity to UKMTO immediately.
- Prepare engine-room firefighting teams, as the strike hit the engine room in the two latest reports.
- Treat reports of mines east of the strait as a potential hazard until an official statement is issued, and increase visual watch for floating objects.
