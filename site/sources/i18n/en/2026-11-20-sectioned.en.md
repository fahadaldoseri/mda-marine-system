## Operational Guidance
**Required actions:**
- Raising readiness when transiting the Strait of Hormuz, especially in the stretch between 6 and 13 nautical miles north of Khasab.
- Reviewing the latest UKMTO reports and update No. 115 of the advisory note before every transit, without relying on media headlines alone.
- Increasing visual watch for floating objects in the strait, and treating reports of mines as a potential hazard until an official statement is issued.
- Reporting any strike, floating object or suspicious activity to UKMTO immediately.
- Following any official warning on the Gulf of Aden and Bab al-Mandab before transiting the southern Red Sea.
