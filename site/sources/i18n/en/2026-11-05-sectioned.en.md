## Executive Summary
- Iran's Revolutionary Guard announced that four oil tankers were destroyed by naval mines inside the Strait of Hormuz, that two vessels were seized and that a United States MQ-9 drone was shot down. The announcement is a party claim without independent confirmation, and repeated headlines from the same outlet are not separate incidents (MN-2026-11-05-01).
- Material cited a United States official saying that United States forces carried out defensive strikes on Iranian targets in the Strait of Hormuz, and another item reported the United States President confirming strikes. No official published statement appears in the material (MN-2026-11-05-02).
- TradeWinds reported that two projectiles struck a very large crude carrier listed this week among vessels banned by Iran, and Seatrade Maritime reported damage to the engine room of a large crude oil tanker in the latest attack in the Strait. The two items most probably describe the vessel recorded on 04/11/2026 as MN-2026-11-04-04 (MN-2026-11-05-03).
- Material stated that Iran will allow Indian ships to pass through the Strait of Hormuz after an eight-month closure (MN-2026-11-05-04). The same material reported that a seafarer from the Indian state of Gujarat was killed in an attack on a vessel near the Strait (MN-2026-11-05-05), and that his family plans burial in Oman. That report is linked to MN-2026-11-04-03.
- No new UKMTO warning was issued during the period; the latest active products remain 194-26 and 195-26 of 03/11/2026.
- The official International Maritime Organization list carried 128 confirmed incidents against commercial ships and 24 confirmed seafarer deaths, read on 4/11/2026 and effective as at 3/11/2026, the list's latest update. No more recent reading appears in the material.
**Operational impact:** risk moved from scattered reports to a party announcing the destruction of four tankers inside the Strait alongside announced United States strikes; because that announcement is a claim without confirmation, routing decisions must rest on official verification first.
Confidence level: low to moderate.
