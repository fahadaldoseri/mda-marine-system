## Operational Guidance
**Required actions:**
- Raising alertness when transiting the Strait of Hormuz, especially between 6 and 17 nautical miles north and east of Khasab, where strikes have recurred.

- Preparing firefighting and damage control equipment in engine rooms before entering the strait.
- Reporting any suspicious activity during transit to UKMTO immediately.
