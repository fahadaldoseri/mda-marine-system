> Maritime Domain Awareness Center — Kingdom of Bahrain · 18/11/2026 · UTC

## Operational Guidance
**Do:**
- Raise readiness when transiting the Strait of Hormuz, especially between Khasab and Ras al-Khaimah.
- Review the latest UKMTO reports before every transit, and do not rely on media headlines alone.
- Report any strike, floating object or suspicious activity to UKMTO immediately.
- Apply engine-room firefighting procedures, as engine-room fires recurred in four official reports.
- Strengthen watchkeeping in the Arabian Sea against armed skiffs, and keep the security team ready.
- Treat reports of mines in the strait as a potential hazard until an official statement is issued.

## Situation Assessment and Indicator Analysis
[Assessment] **Method:** UKMTO report texts were compared with the IMO register, keeping official reports separate from media reports. The strikes in the Arabian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz concentrated on tankers sailing eastbound or exiting the strait. No official report identifies the perpetrator. The situation indicators are read together with the sector card calculated for the day.

**Assumptions and tests of contrary evidence:**
- First assumption: tankers heading out of the Gulf are more exposed to strikes. Contrary evidence: report 204-26 does not state the tanker's heading.
- Second assumption: the strikes are from projectiles, not mines. Contrary evidence: the 18/11 reports of mines, which have not been officially confirmed.
- Third assumption: the two Arabian Sea reports are unrelated to the events in the Gulf. Contrary evidence: there is no official evidence either linking them or ruling out a link.
